About Us

A place to slow down above the city.

Unvind began with a simple idea: Surat should have a place where you can step out of the traffic, climb up to the 6th floor and simply relax. The name says it all.

We keep the space calm and green, with glass walls, warm wood and plenty of plants, so it works for a first coffee, a family lunch or a birthday that goes on past midnight.

The menu is wide on purpose. Pizza, sushi, biryani, smoothie bowls and tiramisu can all sit on one table, because that is how friends actually eat.

  • Rooftop on the 6th floorIndoor and outdoor seating with open-air views.
  • A menu that spans cuisinesItalian, Continental, Indian, Chinese, cafe snacks and desserts.
  • Open late, every dayDoors stay open until 1:00 AM.
  • Made for celebrationsBirthdays, group dinners and casual hangouts are welcome.
  • Dine in or order homeKerbside pickup, no-contact delivery and Swiggy ordering.
